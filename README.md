I'm a **Senior Data Analyst at Nasdaq**, working in trade surveillance and financial markets, with a focus on data quality, market data integration, and solving complex data-related challenges.

I enjoy turning complex datasets into meaningful insights and building practical solutions using **Python, SQL, and Power BI**.

Through my GitHub projects, I share my hands-on learning journey, experiments, and data-driven applications.
